Background

Developer, product manager, remote engineer, open‑source contributor.

I started in 2005 in Beijing, writing VBScript, C# and Java for Japanese customers. In 2008 I joined Idapted, an international English-learning startup, and started building with Ruby on Rails. After it merged with Eleutian in 2010, I was promoted to senior product manager and became the technical bridge for customers in Korea and elsewhere.

In 2011 I joined Intridea and started working remotely with US teams, on more than ten client projects across Rails, Backbone, Angular and D3.js. I also wrote technical articles and presented our engineering practices.

Since 2015 I've mostly worked independently, on Rails and React apps across content, commerce, data and SaaS, with stints at Spire Health and, most recently, Adwave.

These days I build AI features into Rails apps, and I contribute fixes back to the open-source libraries I rely on, like RubyLLM and ActiveAgent.
