Questions

Things hiring teams usually ask.

Are you looking for full-time roles or contract work?

Both are open—full-time remote employment or a long-term direct remote contract with a product team. For teams that aren't ready for an embedded hire yet, I also take on scoped AI & codebase audits and monthly Rails maintenance.

How do you handle time zones from UTC+8?

I've worked remotely with US and European teams since 2011. I default to clear written async updates, small reviewable pull requests and agreed overlap windows for calls and pairing.

If I hire you for a full-time role, what happens to your services?

I only commit to work I can give proper focus to. If I join a team full-time, that role gets my full working week—any client engagements are wound down or scoped cleanly beforehand, never run on the side at the expense of the team.

What stacks are you strongest in?

Ruby on Rails (since 2008), PostgreSQL, background jobs (Solid Queue and Sidekiq), Hotwire, React and TypeScript, and production AI in Ruby/Rails (RubyLLM, ActiveAgent, MCP, structured outputs, tool permissions, evals and cost control).

What is helpful to send in a first email?

The role or problem space, your product and stack, team size and time zones, whether it's full-time or contract, and a link to the job description if you have one.
